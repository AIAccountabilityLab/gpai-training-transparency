Evaluation Notes

If you update your public summary, please let us know so that we can update the evaluation and scores. We also welcome suggestions on improvements to this page, e.g. see FAQ section.

Adobe Firefly provides a generally high-quality summary about their training of a model using large amounts of image and video data. The summary primarily loses points due to a lack of details in some fields and the summary not being disclosed along distribution channels. It received a score of 77.94% with Grade B for transparency and 80.11% with Grade B+ for usefulness.
